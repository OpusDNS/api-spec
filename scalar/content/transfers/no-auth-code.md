# No auth code, with approval

These TLDs need no auth code. OpusDNS requests the transfer. Your current registrar or the domain's holder then approves it, depending on the TLD.

## Steps

1. OpusDNS requests the transfer at the registry.
2. Your current registrar or the domain's holder approves or denies the transfer.

## TLDs

[.co.za](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/co.za), [.lu](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/lu), [.net.za](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/net.za), [.org.za](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/org.za), [.web.za](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/web.za).

## At your registrar

✅ OpusDNS does it for you · ↗️ you do it at your registrar · 👤 the domain's holder approves by email · ➖ the TLD doesn't have it
