# Auth code and approval

Your current registrar gives you the auth code. OpusDNS requests the transfer with it. Your current registrar can then approve or deny the transfer. The registry has no transfer lock.

![Swimlane in three lanes: your current registrar, the registry and OpusDNS. You get the auth code. OpusDNS requests the transfer at the registry. Your current registrar approves or denies it. The registry transfers the domain. The domain is in your OpusDNS account.](/images/transfer-patterns/auth-code-approval.svg)

## Steps

1. Get the auth code from your current registrar.
2. OpusDNS requests the transfer at the registry with the auth code.
3. Your current registrar approves or denies the transfer. Some registrars ask the domain's holder by email.

Which of these steps OpusDNS takes for you depends on your registrar. Its page in [Your registrar](/transfers/overview#your-registrar) shows them.

## TLDs

[.at](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/at), [.it](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/it), [.mx](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/mx).

Each TLD links its page in the TLD Knowledge Base, with its transfer rules, such as its transfer lock and how long a transfer takes.
