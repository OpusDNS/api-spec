# Auth code only

Your current registrar gives you the auth code. OpusDNS requests the transfer with it. The registry completes the transfer without an approval. The registry has no transfer lock.

![Swimlane in three lanes: your current registrar, the registry and OpusDNS. You get the auth code. OpusDNS requests the transfer at the registry. The registry transfers the domain without an approval. The domain is in your OpusDNS account.](/images/transfer-patterns/auth-code.svg)

## Steps

1. Get the auth code from your current registrar.
2. OpusDNS requests the transfer at the registry with the auth code.
3. The registry completes the transfer without an approval.

Which of these steps OpusDNS takes for you depends on your registrar. Its page in [Your registrar](/transfers/overview#your-registrar) shows them.

[.ch](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/ch), [.de](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/de), [.li](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/li), [.no](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/no), [.pl](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/pl), [.pt](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/pt), [.si](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/si).

Each TLD links its page in the TLD Knowledge Base, with its transfer rules, such as its transfer lock and how long a transfer takes.
