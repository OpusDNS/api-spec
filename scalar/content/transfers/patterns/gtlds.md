# gTLDs and most ccTLDs

Your current registrar gives you the auth code. OpusDNS requests the transfer with it. Your current registrar can then approve or deny the transfer. gTLDs follow ICANN's rules for this, and most ccTLDs work the same way.

![Swimlane in three lanes: your current registrar, the registry and OpusDNS. You unlock the domain at your current registrar. You get the auth code. OpusDNS requests the transfer at the registry. Your current registrar approves or denies it. The registry transfers the domain. The domain is in your OpusDNS account.](/images/transfer-patterns/gtlds.svg)

## Steps

1. Remove the transfer lock at your current registrar.
2. Get the auth code from your current registrar.
3. OpusDNS requests the transfer at the registry with the auth code.
4. Your current registrar approves or denies the transfer. Some registrars ask the domain's holder by email. At gTLDs, the registry completes it after five days without an answer. See [How a gTLD transfer works](/transfers/overview#how-a-gtld-transfer-works).

Which of these steps OpusDNS takes for you depends on your registrar. Its page in [Your registrar](/transfers/overview#your-registrar) shows them.
