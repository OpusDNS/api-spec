# Transferring a .at domain

`.at` follows the [gTLDs and most ccTLDs](/transfers/patterns/gtlds) pattern. Its registry has no transfer lock. A transfer doesn't extend the registration. The registry completes a transfer at once.

## Steps

1. Get the auth code from your current registrar.
2. OpusDNS requests the transfer at the registry with the auth code.
3. Your current registrar approves or denies the transfer. At gTLDs, the registry completes it after five days without an answer. See [How a gTLD transfer works](/transfers/overview#how-a-gtld-transfer-works).

## At your registrar

With a connected account, OpusDNS takes some of these steps for you. Which ones depends on your registrar:

✅ OpusDNS does it for you · ↗️ you do it at your registrar · 👤 the domain's holder approves by email · ➖ the TLD doesn't have it
