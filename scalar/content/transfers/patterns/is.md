# Transferring a .is domain

`.is` has no transfer at its registry. OpusDNS can't request one.

ISNIC works without registrars. A `.is` domain changes provider when its admin contact changes at ISNIC. See [ISNIC's FAQ](https://www.isnic.is/en/faq).

To move a `.is` domain to OpusDNS, contact [OpusDNS support](https://support.opusdns.com/).
