# No auth code, with approval

These TLDs need no auth code. OpusDNS requests the transfer. Your current registrar or the domain's holder then approves or denies the transfer, depending on the TLD. The registry has no transfer lock.

![Swimlane in three lanes: your current registrar, the registry and OpusDNS. OpusDNS requests the transfer at the registry. Your current registrar or the domain's holder approves or denies it. The registry transfers the domain. The domain is in your OpusDNS account.](/images/transfer-patterns/no-auth-code.svg)

## Steps

1. OpusDNS requests the transfer at the registry.
2. Your current registrar or the domain's holder approves or denies the transfer, depending on the TLD.

Which of these steps OpusDNS takes for you depends on your registrar. Its page in [Your registrar](/transfers/overview#your-registrar) shows them.

## TLDs

[.co.za](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/co.za), [.lu](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/lu), [.net.za](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/net.za), [.org.za](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/org.za), [.web.za](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/web.za).

The [TLD Knowledge Base](/tld-knowledge-base) has each TLD's transfer rules, such as its transfer lock and how long a transfer takes.

The registries of these TLDs:

- [ZARC](https://zarc.web.za): [.co.za](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/co.za), [.net.za](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/net.za), [.org.za](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/org.za), [.web.za](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/web.za)
- [Fondation RESTENA (DNS-LU)](https://www.dns.lu): [.lu](/tld-knowledge-base/cctlds/lu)
