# Transferring a .net.za domain

`.net.za` follows the [No auth code, with approval](/transfers/patterns/no-auth-code) pattern. Its registry is [ZARC](https://zarc.web.za). The registry has no transfer lock. A transfer doesn't extend the registration. A transfer takes up to 5 days.

## Steps

1. OpusDNS requests the transfer at the registry.
2. Your current registrar approves or denies the transfer. Some registrars ask the domain's holder by email.

## At your registrar
